## 1. Problem summary

The debt recovery systems of Legacy Trust are tracked by separate spreadsheets, email trails and legacy collections databases. The fragmented approach has gaps, relies on individual memory, leads to extraneous, duplicated effort and reduced visibility. The gaps are patched by basic workarounds, so the growth of operations, leading to now 100,000 delinquent accounts, has outpaced the current systems. The duplicated efforts of 50 representatives, and lack of visibility into follow-ups is leading to wastes of employee time, and missed recoveries that slip through gaps. The efforts are being allocated to tasks where they are not most beneficial, which is a huge opportunity cost. We need to quantify these costs, so that we can target a self-service solution in the right areas, where automation could reduce duplication. Leadership will need to see evidence that a proposed solution would solve these problems, and evidence that the problems are leading to the costs where we say they are.

## 3. Discovery questions


1. Capacity & Handling Time (Amina / Daniel): What is the average handling time spent per case across the 50 representatives, and what proportion of that time goes toward manual administrative work (e.g., checking spreadsheets, status reconciliation)? 

2. Repetition & Root Causes (Gareth): Which specific recovery steps or tasks generate the most duplicate outreach and repeated customer contacts?

3. Data Quality & Friction (Gareth / Amina): What case details are most frequently missing or incomplete in the legacy database, and how does that affect follow-up accuracy?

4. Automation Suitability (Priya / Amina): Which routine interactions (e.g., balance checks, standard payment plans) account for the highest volume and are most viable for customer self-service?

5. Complexity & Exception Triage (Amina / Gareth): What specific factors (e.g., product type like auto finance vs. credit card, dispute flags) make a case too complex for self-service, and what percentage of the 100,000 accounts fit this profile?

6. Financial & Revenue Baseline (Daniel): What is the total monetary value of the 100,000 delinquent accounts, and what baseline metrics underpin the estimated 15% revenue loss? How expensive is agent time?

7. Technical & Compliance Safeguards (Daniel / Priya): What legal or technical constraints (e.g., regulatory outreach limits, legacy system sync rules) must a self-service portal adhere to in Phase 1?
