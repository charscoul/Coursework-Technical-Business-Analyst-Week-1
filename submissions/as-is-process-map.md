## Current process feelings

*Customer* 
- Frustrated with repeated requests for the same information
- Frustrated by missed follow-ups
- Frustrated by long resolution times

*Collections Representative*
- Frustrated with duplicated work
- Frustrated by working on complex cases that require escalation
- Frustrated by inaccurate information, having to check multiple sources, and inconsistent case status definitions

*Team Leader*
- Frustrated with lack of visibility into team performance
- Frustrated by inconsistent case status definitions and the need for extensive reconciliation
- Frustrated by having to retrieve full context for escalated cases

## Automation candidates
- Updating the spreadsheet with new cases
- Prioritising cases (including skipping customers recently contacted / separating customers on their final chance before escalation / prioritising customers with scheduled follow-ups)
- Compiling database and spreadsheet information
- Updating contact status
- Logging outcomes in multiple locations based on single entry
- Automating follow-up reminders based on case status
- Assigning cases during shift hand-offs
- Updating case information before hand-offs
- First contact with newly delinquent cases
